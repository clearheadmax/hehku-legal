hehku

Privacy Policy · Last updated 2 November 2026

Hehku is a guided Finnish sauna and cold-plunge app made by Maximilian Opas in Finland. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your sessions and health data stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your sessions, streaks, ritual and program progress, and settings are stored only on your iPhone. We do not have servers that receive them, and we cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Hehku:

  • saves your sauna and cold-plunge sessions to Apple Health as workouts, and
  • reads your heart rate during sessions, and your heart rate variability, resting heart rate and sleep, to show how your sessions line up with your recovery.

This health information is read and processed only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, never used for advertising, and never sold. You can change or withdraw access at any time in the iPhone Settings app under Health.

Purchases

Hehku Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. To check whether you have an active subscription, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app identifier and your App Store purchase information, and nothing about your health or sessions. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or third-party tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.

Children

Hehku is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because your data lives on your device, you control it: you can delete it by deleting the app, and manage Health access in Settings. If you have questions or want to exercise any rights under applicable law (such as the GDPR), contact us.

Health information

Hehku tracks habits and shows patterns in your own data. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Check with a doctor before using sauna or cold water if you have a heart condition or other health concerns.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
